Dual-Mode-RAG-Sys

                

                    I built this project to solve the dilemma between data privacy and model performance. Many RAG systems force you to choose one: send your private data to the cloud OR suffer slow, lower-quality responses locally.Dual-Mode RAG Sys gives you the best of both worlds. It processes documents locally using Qdrant and LangChain, but gives the user a simple toggle switch to decide who answers the question:

🔒 Local Mode: Uses Mistral (via Ollama) running entirely on your machine. Zero data leaves your network.

⚡ Cloud Mode: Uses Gemini 2.5 Flash for complex reasoning and faster responses when privacy is less critical.
